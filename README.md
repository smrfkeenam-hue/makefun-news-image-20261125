One image summing up the world news of **Wednesday 25 November 2026**,
drawn as a newspaper front page with six stories:

1. **America waits on inflation** — US PCE inflation and Q3 GDP due today;
   a December Fed hike is ~95% priced in.
2. **Kyiv admits ship strikes** — Zelenskyy confirms Ukrainian drone hits on
   Russian ships in the Gulf of Finland and off Benghazi, Libya.
3. **Climate talks collapse** — COP31 ends in Antalya with no deal on
   fossil fuels.
4. **Huang back at Nvidia** — shares jump 31% as the founder returns as CEO.
5. **Gold & Bitcoin slide** — gold near $3,550; Bitcoin under $60,000.
6. **Thanksgiving eve** — Trump pardons turkeys Freedom and Feathers.

`main.py` (stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory.
